Who does what?
01 · Local government

Local roads, drains, council facilities and local planning. Supports community recovery.

02 · State government

Leads emergency management. Coordinates emergency services. Manages public hospitals, state roads and government schools.

03 · Federal government

Supports states with national coordination, extra resources and disaster financial assistance.

Responsibilities can overlap. Councils work with emergency agencies within state arrangements. Federal support does not automatically mean taking over.
